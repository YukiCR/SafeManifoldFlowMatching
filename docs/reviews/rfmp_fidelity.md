# RFMP fidelity review

29 October 2026. Independent review of `methodology.md`, before specification or tickets. Primary material checked directly: [original v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2403.10672v1), [original v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2403.10672v2), and [author-hosted PDF](https://h2t.iar.kit.edu/pdf/Braun2024.pdf). Their relevant LASA method descriptions agree. The supplied `stable_rfmp.txt` was checked only to distinguish the later work. No implementation, data preparation, training or experiments were performed. Findings are recommendations, not applicant decisions.

**Verdict: conditionally sound method; no need to replace the approved two-phase direction.** Sections 3–4 preserve the original method's essential structure. The unresolved details prevent an exact reproduction claim today, but generally belong in the later specification. Make the horizon ambiguity and conditioning contract explicit before approving an implementable specification. The document already labels unfinished work honestly.

**Verified fidelity.** Position-valued action chunks, repeated-point source, history conditioning, geodesic regression and Riemannian Euler inference match original §§II-B–IV-A. The reported MLP, Swish, Adam, EMA, epoch count and split percentages in methodology lines 45–54 are consistent with §§III-C–IV-A. No hard endpoint pinning is reported in that method. [Original RFMP](https://arxiv.org/html/2403.10672v2#S3).

Prioritized findings follow.

1. **P1 — Record the horizon inconsistency explicitly; do not silently resolve it as an author fact.** Affected: §4, lines 47 and 62; §3, line 39; §6, line 126. The paper's inclusive vectors imply nine predicted and five executed entries at the stated horizons; its prose instead calls the latter the first four actions. [RFMP §§III-A/B and IV-A](https://arxiv.org/html/2403.10672v2#S3). Thus the current “point-count versus transition-count” caveat is warranted, but understates the concrete disagreement. The later specification should define the exact target indices, executed indices and next rollout index together, identify the adopted interpretation and preserve it in both phases. A disclosed interpretation is reasonable; claiming verified original 8/4 array lengths is not. This is an implementation gate, not evidence that short-chunk RFMP is unsuitable.

2. **P1 — State the complete conditioning contract now; leave bootstrap mechanics to specification.** Affected: §3, lines 30–37; §4, lines 46 and 62. Original §III-A specifies `c ~ Uniform{1,...,τ−2}` and `[o_(τ−1),o_c,τ−c]`; §IV-A pairs them with the same demonstration and prediction index. [RFMP conditioning and dataset construction](https://arxiv.org/html/2403.10672v2#S3.SS1). Add that explicit range and pairing, and say that context is drawn once per generation solve. Otherwise an implementer could independently sample unrelated observations or replace random historical context with the last two adjacent states. The latter also risks importing the later paper's observation-window convention. Initial history, availability of sufficiently early observations, and treatment near demonstration ends remain ordinary specification choices. Generated rollout history must supply later observations; consulting future demonstrated positions would undermine the intended rollout evaluation.

3. **P2 — Add two paper ambiguities to the reproduction-gap list.** Affected: §4, lines 48, 50, 56 and 62. Original §III-C's input description omits the evolving action state despite its field equations; its prior notation uses `σI`. [RFMP implementation](https://arxiv.org/html/2403.10672v2#S3.SS3). Keep the present state-dependent `V(s,X|o)` formulation. Explicitly disclose that the specification must determine the MLP's full input/output shapes and the Gaussian parameter convention. In particular, tangent covariance `0.5 I` and tangent standard deviation `0.5` produce different priors. Do not let the vague word “scale” silently choose one. Layer counting, batch size, epoch definition and validation score/EMA-checkpoint handling also need operational definitions; the reported hyperparameters alone do not fix them.

4. **P2 — Strengthen the validation contract without expanding the project.** Affected: §4, lines 62–68; §7, lines 136–140. The existing discussion correctly distinguishes seven recordings from many dependent examples. Preserve that distinction in every eventual result caption. A split after expansion across context choices can place an identical future chunk in different partitions with different contexts; even a target-window split can share most underlying positions across partitions. Record the split unit and overlap explicitly, fit preprocessing on training information, and keep evaluation data out of scenario/tuning choices. A sample-level validation score can support model selection under its stated protocol; it cannot establish unseen-demonstration generalization. Neither a successful safety filter nor unit norms alone validate that Phase 1 learned the motion. Add a minimal baseline gate requiring an explicitly chosen reproduction-quality measure and a complete nominal rollout assessment before freezing. Numerical thresholds belong in the specification.

**Geometry, endpoints and phase separation.** The product-of-spheres construction and tangent projection at line 56 are appropriate mathematical specializations of the selected chunk model. Regressing the derivative along the source-to-data geodesic is consistent with original Eqs. (6)–(8). [RFMP geometry](https://arxiv.org/html/2403.10672v2#S2.SS2). Equality to a demonstrated sample at the end of a training interpolation supplies no hard goal-reaching property to a learned physical rollout. Retain lines 54 and 140. Preserve the current Phase 1/Phase 2 boundary: safety activation, cone margins and any changed integration protocol should be recorded as Phase 2 choices alongside the unchanged baseline.

**No later-method substitution found.** The later Ding–Jaquier–Peters–Rozo work uses a UNet and a finite observation window, and introduces an autonomous augmented-state stable method; these are distinct from this original-paper reproduction. [Later paper §§IV–V-A](https://arxiv.org/html/2412.10855v3). Methodology lines 12, 26, 50 and 58 currently resist that substitution correctly. Keep SAD-Flower's recovery mechanism confined to the safety extension. Neither later paper can retrospectively establish an undocumented original LASA implementation detail.

**What remains unverified.** No authoritative original LASA training/sampling implementation was established in this focused review. Exact normalization/spherical embedding, data resampling, split unit, padding, bootstrap, MLP layer convention, batching and solver resolution remain unverified. The author PDF and v2 do not resolve the listed horizon/input ambiguities. I did not audit the dataset license, repository-wide absence of old artifacts, or the Phase 2 safety theorem. The document's statements that no new experiments have run remain status declarations, not independently verified experimental evidence.

**Next gate.** The applicant can review and accept this methodology with the minimal clarifications above. Afterwards a specification may select and label unresolved reproduction choices; no implementation should proceed until the user's required specification/ticket approvals. Until measured results exist, describe the work as a planned original-RFMP reimplementation on one family, followed by a proposed safety extension.
